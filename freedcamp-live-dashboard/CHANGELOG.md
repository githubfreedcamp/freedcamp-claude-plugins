# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-11-20)

New "Work on it" button: hand a task to Claude from the dashboard, and Claude works on it in your Claude Code session and posts progress on the task as comments.

- "Work on it" is the first option in "Help with this", and is on check-in nudges and Plan my day cards.
- Tasks Claude is working on show a "Claude working" pill.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-20)

Dashboard buttons now act on the page itself: "Help with this", Confirm, offers and nudges save straight to Freedcamp after you confirm, with no copy and paste.

- "Help with this" on every task: Claude drafts a description, comment, subtasks, an assignee or a plan, and saves it after you confirm.
- "Apply my day" checks the suggested tasks; suggested rows start checked with a reason.
- Confirm marks your picked tasks In progress in Freedcamp.
- Check-in and Helpers sections, with quick status buttons on each nudge.
- Plan my day cards get "Defer" and an "I could take" list.
- A one-time terminal notice when the plugin is updated.
