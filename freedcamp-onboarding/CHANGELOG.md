# Changelog

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-20)

"Work on it": tasks you hand to Claude from the dashboard get picked up on the next check-in, worked on by a helper, and updated with progress comments in Freedcamp until there is a result.

- The heartbeat finds "[Claude: work on it]" requests, starts a helper, and posts started, progress and done comments on the task.
- Status, description, subtask and assignee changes still wait for your yes.
- Dashboard: "Work on it" in "Help with this", check-in nudges and Plan my day cards.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-20)

Friendly check-ins that ask how your focus tasks are going and report helper progress, a fresh dashboard link on every tick, and dashboard buttons that act on the page itself.

- Each check-in nudges up to 3 focus tasks ("Hey, how's Write brief going?") with a quick status question.
- "Here's what I've been working on with the helpers you asked for": researcher and project manager progress, recorded in memory.md.
- A fresh board every tick, ending with "Board: <url>"; one short line only when there is truly nothing.
- Dashboard: "Help with this", "Apply my day", suggested picks, and Confirm that saves to Freedcamp, all on the page.
- Onboarding interview with clickable questions, and a dashboard published at the end.
- A one-time terminal notice when the plugin is updated.
